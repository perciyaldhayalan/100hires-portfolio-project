# Overview

The purpose of this section is not to criticize any SEO professional. Every expert researched for this project has contributed valuable knowledge to the SEO community. However, based on the objectives of this playbook—sustainable, user-focused, AI-assisted SEO—some approaches are less suitable than others.

The following evaluation is based on how well each expert's recommendations align with the long-term principles adopted throughout this playbook.

---

# Expert I Would Not Primarily Recommend

## Matt Diggity

### Background

Matt Diggity is a well-known SEO practitioner recognized for his practical experiments, affiliate SEO experience, and advanced ranking strategies. His content often focuses on competitive niches, backlinks, testing, and rapid SEO experimentation.

### Sources

- *I Did AI SEO for ChatGPT and Google AI. Here's What Happened...*
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4GBlHObjOrY

- Research notes from this repository.

---

## Why I Would Not Recommend Following Him as a Primary Learning Resource

This decision is **not** because his advice is incorrect. Many of his experiments provide valuable insights.

However, I believe his strategies are better suited to experienced SEO professionals than beginners or organizations seeking sustainable long-term growth.

### Reason 1 – Heavy Focus on Competitive Tactics

Many examples emphasize competitive ranking strategies that require significant SEO experience to execute safely.

### Reason 2 – Risk of Misapplication

Less experienced practitioners may copy tactical recommendations without understanding the broader SEO strategy, leading to poor results.

### Reason 3 – Limited Emphasis on Editorial Quality

Compared with experts such as Lily Ray, Andy Crestodina, and Aleyda Solis, there is less emphasis on editorial governance, user trust, and E-E-A-T as the primary foundation.

# My Perspective

The goal of this playbook is not to achieve quick ranking improvements through isolated tactics.

Instead, it focuses on:

- Helpful content
- Human expertise
- Responsible AI
- Long-term authority
- Continuous improvement

For these reasons, I believe beginners and organizations should build their foundation using the approaches promoted by Lily Ray, Andy Crestodina, Kevin Indig, Brian Dean, and Aleyda Solis before exploring more advanced experimental strategies.

---

# Conclusion

Every expert researched in this project has valuable experience and unique perspectives. However, if I had to recommend one expert **not** to follow as a primary learning resource for this playbook, it would be **Matt Diggity**.

This recommendation reflects the objectives of this playbook rather than the quality of his work. His material remains valuable for advanced practitioners, but I believe a sustainable, user-first approach provides a stronger foundation for most teams and individuals adopting AI-assisted SEO.
