# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## AI-Powered SEO Content Production

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines a repeatable workflow for planning, researching, creating, reviewing, optimizing, publishing, and maintaining AI-assisted SEO content. It combines Artificial Intelligence with human expertise to ensure every published article is accurate, valuable, search-engine friendly, and aligned with Google's E-E-A-T principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Blog Articles
- Landing Pages
- Knowledge Base Articles
- Product Pages
- Evergreen Content
- Educational Content
- AI-assisted SEO Content

---

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| SEO Specialist | Keyword research, search intent analysis, SEO optimization |
| Content Strategist | Topic planning and content strategy |
| Content Writer | Draft and refine content |
| AI Assistant | Generate outlines, ideas, summaries, first drafts |
| Editor | Fact-checking, readability, quality assurance |
| Publisher | Publish, index, monitor performance |

# 5. Step 1 – Topic Selection

## Objective

Select a topic that aligns with business objectives and user needs.

## Inputs

- Business goals
- Target audience
- Existing content
- Industry trends

## Procedure

1. Define the target audience.
2. Identify common customer problems.
3. Brainstorm relevant topics.
4. Validate demand using search data.
5. Prioritize topics based on value and relevance.

## Deliverables

- Approved topic
- Audience definition
- Content brief

## Quality Checklist

- User-focused
- Relevant
- Valuable
- Supports business objectives

- Andy Crestodina
- Rand Fishkin

# 6. Step 2 – Keyword Research

## Objective

Identify keywords that reflect user intent and business goals.

## Procedure

1. Select one primary keyword.
2. Identify supporting keywords.
3. Collect long-tail keywords.
4. Review "People Also Ask".
5. Identify semantic entities.
6. Record search intent.

## Deliverables

- Keyword list
- Search intent report

## Quality Checklist

- Relevant
- Achievable
- Intent aligned

**Supported Experts**

- Brian Dean
- Neil Patel

---

# 7. Step 3 – Search Intent Analysis

## Objective

Understand what users expect before writing.

## Procedure

1. Search the keyword.
2. Review the top 10 results.
3. Identify dominant intent.
4. Analyze headings and FAQs.
5. Identify content gaps.

## Deliverables

- Intent classification
- Competitor observations

**Supported Experts**

- Brian Dean
- Kevin Indig

# 8. Step 4 – Competitor Research

## Objective

## Procedure

  - Images
  - Internal links
  - FAQs
  - User experience

## Deliverables

- Competitor comparison sheet

**Supported Experts**

# 9. Step 5 – AI-Assisted Content Planning

## Objective

Use AI responsibly to accelerate planning.

## Procedure

1. Generate outlines.
2. Compare multiple versions.
3. Remove generic sections.
4. Add original insights.
5. Approve outline.

## Deliverables

- Final content outline

## AI Usage Guidelines

Use AI for:

- Brainstorming
- Summaries
- Headings
- Drafts

Do not rely on AI for:

- Facts
- Statistics
- Citations
- Final publishing

- Mike King
- Lily Ray

# 15. Quality Assurance Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- Topic matches user intent
- Facts verified
- Grammar checked
- AI output reviewed
- SEO elements completed
- Internal links added
- External references added
- Original insights included
- Mobile readability checked

---

# 16. Success Metrics

Success should be measured by:

- Organic growth
- User engagement
- Conversions
- Returning visitors
- Search visibility
- Content freshness

---

# 17. Continuous Improvement

SEO is an ongoing process.

Every published article should be reviewed periodically to:

- Update outdated information
- Improve internal linking
- Expand content
- Refresh examples
- Optimize for evolving search behavior
- Adapt to AI search developments
