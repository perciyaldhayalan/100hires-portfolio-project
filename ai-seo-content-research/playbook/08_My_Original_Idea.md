## AI-Powered SEO Content Production

# Overview

The following framework is my original contribution to this playbook. During my research, I found many recommendations for using AI to generate content, optimize pages, or improve productivity. However, I did not find a practical method for evaluating whether AI-assisted content is truly ready for publication.

To address this gap, I propose the **AI Content Confidence Framework (AICCF)**.

---

# AI Content Confidence Framework (AICCF)

## Purpose

The framework provides a structured review process that every AI-assisted article must pass before publication. Instead of asking "Is this article finished?", the team asks, "How confident are we that this article is accurate, helpful, and trustworthy?"

Each article receives a confidence score based on measurable review criteria.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

| Category | Description | Score |
|----------|-------------|------:|
| Accuracy | Facts, statistics, and claims are verified | 20 |
| User Intent | Content completely satisfies search intent | 20 |
| Original Value | Includes unique insights, examples, or analysis | 20 |
| E-E-A-T | Demonstrates experience, expertise, authority, and trust | 20 |
| Readability | Clear structure, grammar, and formatting | 10 |
| SEO Optimization | Metadata, internal links, headings, and images | 10 |

**Maximum Score:** 100

---

# Confidence Levels

| Score | Recommendation |
|-------:|----------------|
| 90–100 | Ready for publication |
| 75–89 | Minor revisions required |
| 60–74 | Major editorial review required |
| Below 60 | Do not publish |

---

# Review Workflow

```text
AI Draft
   ↓
Human Editor Review
   ↓
Fact Verification
   ↓
AICCF Scoring
   ↓
Final Approval
   ↓
Publish
```

---

# Why This Could Work

Most AI workflows focus on generating content quickly, but they do not provide a consistent quality gate before publishing.

This framework introduces measurable quality control by:

- Encouraging human accountability.
- Reducing factual errors.
- Improving consistency across articles.
- Reinforcing Google's E-E-A-T principles.
- Helping teams make objective publishing decisions.

---

# Example

An AI-assisted article receives:

- Accuracy: 18/20
- User Intent: 20/20
- Original Value: 15/20
- E-E-A-T: 18/20
- Readability: 9/10
- SEO Optimization: 9/10

**Final Score:** 89/100

Recommendation: Minor revisions before publishing.

---

# Why I Believe This Adds Value

The experts I researched consistently recommend human review, fact-checking, and quality content. My contribution is turning those recommendations into a practical scoring system that teams can apply consistently.

Rather than relying on subjective opinions, editors can evaluate every article using the same measurable criteria. This improves consistency, reduces publishing risk, and encourages continuous improvement.

---

# Limitations

The framework still depends on experienced reviewers to assign scores fairly. It should be treated as a decision-support tool rather than a replacement for editorial judgment.

The AI Content Confidence Framework extends the ideas presented by the researched experts into a practical operational model.
