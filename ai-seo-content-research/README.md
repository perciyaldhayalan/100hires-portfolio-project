# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Research & Playbook

## Project Overview

This repository documents my research into **AI-Powered SEO Content Production** and transforms that research into a practical playbook and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).

The objective was not only to collect information from leading SEO experts, but also to evaluate differing viewpoints, apply independent judgment, and develop a repeatable workflow for creating high-quality AI-assisted SEO content.

---

# Project Objectives

- Research modern AI-assisted SEO practices.
- Compare recommendations from leading SEO experts.
- Identify areas of agreement and disagreement.
- Build a practical SEO Playbook and SOP.
- Contribute original ideas supported by critical analysis.

# Research Methodology

The research followed four stages:

1. Collect expert resources from YouTube, LinkedIn, blogs, and articles.
2. Summarize key insights and observations.
3. Compare recommendations across experts.
4. Build an original AI SEO Playbook supported by evidence and personal evaluation.
