# AI SEO Content Research

This project collects and organizes research about AI-powered SEO content production from well-known SEO practitioners. It includes YouTube transcript notes, LinkedIn post research, selected source documentation, and observations about how AI search is changing content strategy, visibility, authority, and measurement.

## Project Contents

- `research/sources.md` lists the selected SEO experts, why they were chosen, and the research focus for each person.
- `research/youtube-transcripts/` contains transcript-based research notes from expert videos and interviews.
- `research/linkedin-posts/` captures LinkedIn-based insights from the same group of practitioners.
- `research/other/` includes supporting observations and tools related to AI SEO research.

## Research Focus

The research is centered on how SEO teams can adapt content production for AI-powered search systems such as AI Overviews, LLM answer engines, and generative search experiences. Key themes include content quality, topical authority, entity recognition, crawlability, citations, brand visibility, and the balance between AI-assisted workflows and human expertise.

## Usage

Use this repository as a structured research base for analyzing expert perspectives, comparing SEO strategies, and identifying practical patterns for AI-driven content planning and optimization.
