# LinkedIn Posts — Brian Dean

## Post 1

**Date:** 2025 (exact date not specified in source)

**Topic:** Why chasing LLM/AI-checklist tactics without strategy fails

**Summary:**
Brian Dean criticized the trend of SEOs mechanically following checklist tactics for LLM visibility (mentioning a brand a set number of times on Reddit, adding FAQs, hitting a target number of brand mentions) without first asking why they are doing it. He argued the real question should be the underlying goal — visibility, brand awareness, or being positioned as the right solution to a searcher's problem — since tactics without a clear goal rarely work.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/brianedean (post referenced via Exploding Topics LinkedIn activity)

**Relevance to research:**
Reinforces a theme found across multiple experts in this research: AI/LLM visibility tactics are only effective when grounded in a clear strategic goal, not applied as a generic checklist.

## Post 2

**Date:** Recent (Backlinko company LinkedIn page, referencing Google I/O 2026)

**Topic:** AI Overviews citations declining, and the shift toward owned brand presence

**Summary:**
A Backlinko company post observed that during Google I/O 2026 demos, AI Overviews showed barely any citations, suggesting that optimizing purely for AI Overview citations may already be a fading strategy. The post argued the better long-term move is building a brand people search for by name directly, growing an email list people actually open, and tracking share of voice across multiple platforms (not just Google), since Google's own reporting only covers its own surfaces and misses visibility on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/backlinko

**Relevance to research:**
Adds a more skeptical, platform-independent angle to the AI-visibility conversation — contrasts with Neil Patel's framing of AI visibility as a fast-closing opportunity by suggesting that some forms of AI-citation optimization may already be losing relevance, and that owned brand equity is the safer long-term bet.
