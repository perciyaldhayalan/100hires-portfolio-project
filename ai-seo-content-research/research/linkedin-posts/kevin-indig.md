## Post 1

**Date:** 2026 (How to redirect your SEO budget for 2026)

**Topic:** Reallocating SEO budget toward brand authority instead of clicks

**Summary:**
Kevin Indig argued that marketing leaders need to stop funding SEO purely for clicks and instead design budgets around building brand authority in AI-first search. The post frames this as a necessary shift in how SEO investment is justified internally, moving away from traffic-based ROI arguments.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_digital-marketing-budgets-are-growing-725-activity-7393679413621473280-3eEn

## Post 2

**Date:** Recent (referenced via Search Engine Journal author bio)

**Topic:** AI search breaking the link between traffic and revenue

**Summary:**
Kevin Indig explored how AI search has decoupled traffic from revenue — meaning SEO can create demand and influence purchase decisions without generating measurable page views, since users may never click through despite being influenced by content visible in AI answers.

**Source:** https://www.searchenginejournal.com/author/kevin-indig/

**Relevance to research:**
Extends the video's "engagement model" prediction — supports the idea that a website's value can no longer be judged by click volume alone, and that demand generation now partly happens invisibly inside AI answers.
