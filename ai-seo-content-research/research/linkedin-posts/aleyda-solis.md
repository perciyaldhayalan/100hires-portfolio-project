## Post 1

**Date:** May 31, 2026

**Topic:** Screaming Frog v24 MCP integration for AI-assisted SEO workflows

**Summary:**
Aleyda Solis highlighted Screaming Frog v24.0 as one of the AI and SEO integrations she finds genuinely useful. The update shipped an official MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, allowing AI tools like Claude to connect directly to Screaming Frog and trigger crawls, retrieve data, and build reports through natural language — without manual CSV exports. Aleyda noted that this shifts the mechanical parts of an audit (pulling data, merging sources, formatting reports) to AI, freeing the practitioner's time for higher-judgment decisions. She also flagged two cautions: API credit costs add up when running prompts across multiple MCP-connected tools simultaneously, and all AI output still needs to be validated before acting on it.

## Post 2

**Date:** June 8, 2026

**Topic:** How to build a representative AI search prompt library for accurate visibility measurement

**Summary:**
Aleyda published a detailed framework on LinkedIn for building a structured AI search prompt library — addressing what she calls one of the most common measurement errors in AI search: using the wrong prompts to begin with. Her argument is that if the prompt set is not representative of the actual business, audience, and customer journey, the visibility data it produces is not accurate. She outlined a process starting from business questions rather than prompts, mapping segmentation layers (product lines, audiences, journey stages, markets), using real buyer language from multiple sources, and organizing prompts into groups rather than isolated queries. She recommended starting with 30 to 50 prompts and running them repeatedly across platforms with a fixed sampling protocol to measure defensibly. Visibility gaps should be mapped to one of three causes: a content gap, a source credibility issue, or an entity recognition problem.

**Relevance to research:**
