# LinkedIn Posts — Rand Fishkin

## Post 1

**Date:** September 17, 2025

**Topic:** New SEO vs classic SEO — different skills, not harder

**Summary:**
Rand Fishkin argued that appearing in AI Overviews, LLM responses, and sources of audience influence in 2025 is not harder than ranking in Google in 2015 — it is simply different. He framed the shift as moving away from keyword and link optimization toward getting your brand mentioned in the right documents across the web, requiring more creativity and broader skills in entity optimization, structured data, and brand mentions.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_is-new-seo-harder-than-classic-seo-ie-activity-7374213613286465536-tZbS

**Relevance to research:**
Directly supports the video's theme that AI visibility requires a different approach than traditional SEO — not more effort, but a different kind of effort focused on brand presence across platforms rather than keyword rankings.

## Post 2

**Date:** June 5, 2025

**Topic:** AI tools still represent a tiny fraction of search volume compared to Google

**Summary:**
Rand Fishkin shared data from the Datos Q1 2025 State of Search report showing that only 0.55% of all web visits go to AI tools, compared to 10.55% going to traditional search engines. He acknowledged AI's real and significant growth but cautioned against overstating its current scale, pushing back on the dominant narrative that AI tools are already replacing Google at meaningful volume.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_what-did-people-do-after-they-searched-google-activity-7336469320325050368-rkk3

**Relevance to research:**
Balances the video's AI visibility focus with a grounding data point — AI search is a real and growing opportunity, but its current share of traffic is still small, which reinforces Rand's point that the opportunity is early and underpriced rather than already dominant.
