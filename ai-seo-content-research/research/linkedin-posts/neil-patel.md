# LinkedIn Posts — Neil Patel

## Post 1

**Date:** September 6, 2025

**Topic:** Ranking on Google does not guarantee visibility in ChatGPT

**Summary:**
Neil Patel shared a study finding that ranking on Google's first page does not guarantee appearing in ChatGPT's answers, citing a 38% chance of being completely invisible there despite high Google rankings. He framed this as a shift from SEO to "GEO" (Generative Engine Optimization), urging brands to audit their AI visibility and adapt their content strategy.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_ai-seo-digitalmarketing-activity-7370063368121913344-1hqr

**Relevance to research:**
Directly supports the video's core argument that traditional ranking success does not translate automatically into AI visibility, reinforcing the need for a separate AI-focused content strategy.

## Post 2

**Date:** September 17, 2025

**Topic:** AI search becoming an ad-driven ecosystem

**Summary:**
Neil Patel argued that AI search platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google) are following the same path as past free platforms, eventually evolving into ad-driven ecosystems. He pointed to OpenAI's valuation pressures and Google's shifting incentives as evidence that the "attention economy" is shifting toward AI-mediated answers, and that brands need to compete to be the cited answer rather than just chasing rankings.

**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_ai-search-is-no-longer-the-future-its-already-activity-7374057078186926081-uk5O

**Relevance to research:**
Connects to the video's "AI as gatekeeper" framing and reinforces the idea that AI visibility is a fast-closing window of opportunity rather than a permanent advantage.
